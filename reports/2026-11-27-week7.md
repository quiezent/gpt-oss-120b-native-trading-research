## What we learned

1. **The size of a book at the close decides the week.** gpt-oss sleeves hold about 3x gross at every close and don't weigh the night: their notes mention it in about 1 of 540 looks, against about 64% for Fable and half or more for Inkling. 0 of 33 Friday last looks changed a book for the weekend. Monday's 5% gap cost the carried books 17–25%. The one lever with evidence is a term told in the system text: since the 3.0x sentence, every gpt-oss sleeve has ended every day at 3.3x or less, with no software check.
2. **Where a look decides what it trades.** The names and direction are chosen in the research turns, before the draft. Training on the scanner turns moved the scans; instructions at the drafting turn moved nothing. Whether it moves the entries is the open question.
3. **The trained candidates were not worse than base this week.** That's the first week of seven. One week is within the spread base shows against itself.
4. **No new data source gave direction.** The new IBKR series failed every direction test after costs. They did show when the overnight gap arrives, which shapes December's 09:00 look.
5. **Holidays and outages need their own rules.** A half-day close, a Thursday holiday and an afternoon with no link all fell in one week, and each needed a hand step (Incidents).
