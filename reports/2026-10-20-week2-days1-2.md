# Week 2, days 1–2: six sleeves against cash and SPY (19–20 October 2026)

*Trader Training, written 21 October 2026. The full week reports after Friday's close.*

## Setup

- **Six sleeves of $30,000**, fresh on Monday 19 October: three models, each run twice.
  - **base:** gpt-oss-120b, untrained, reasoning effort high.
  - **rl3:** gpt-oss-120b with our LoRA, step 8 (replaces week 1's rl1; see the [training report](2026-10-18-training-rl1-to-rl3.md)), effort high.
  - **opus:** Claude Opus 5.5, effort max.
  - **"+ forecast"** twins (base-ml, rl3-ml, opus-ml in our logs) get one extra research tool, the ML risk and cost forecasts described in the [ML report](2026-10-19-ml-forecast-tool.md). Comparing each pair measures what the tool adds.
- **14 looks a day**, every 30 minutes from 09:00 to 15:30 New York time. The 09:00 look on 19 Oct was held while the account reset was checked.
- **Benchmarks:** $30,000 kept in cash, or put into SPY at the 16 Oct close ($763.27).

## Behaviour

- **Opus changed most from week 1.** It read the most full articles (56 and 65 over two days, against 3 or fewer for every gpt-oss sleeve), then took large positions: 1,320 CLF shares on day 1, mostly sold on day 2 for a $1,227 realized loss, plus shorts in XLP, XLU, GM, GE and GPC. Week 1's "patient allocator" label did not survive a faster cadence and maximum effort.
- **Base** kept trading the most and ended day 2 net short (about −0.74× of its value). Its largest realized loss was TQQQ (−$425).
- **rl3** leaned on the scanner (82 scans in two days) and held 15–16 names on borrowed cash.
- **Opus + forecast** was the calmest sleeve: 40% fewer orders than Opus and 61% lower commissions.
- **Positions at the 20 Oct close** ranged from net short (Base) to about 1.9× long on borrowed cash (Opus + forecast), whose book was SMH, XLV, EFX, MMM and HAS.

## Incidents

- **Orphan positions from the reset.** Clayton reset the account to $250,000 over the weekend, but IB Gateway still showed the old positions, so the TWS Gateway Owner's 30 closing orders at Monday's open opened 30 reverse positions instead. The TWS Gateway Owner closed them at Tuesday's open. They belonged to no sleeve; the round trip cost the account $1,767 and did not touch any sleeve's results.
- **Lost fills.** IB Gateway serves executions only since midnight UTC+8 (the Gateway's login time zone), which is noon New York time. Nine fills from the 11:30 look were never delivered. We rebuilt them from IBKR's trade confirmation. The loop now syncs fills 5 seconds after each look and 3 minutes before that cutoff, and reconciles positions at every look. The TWS Gateway Owner found the cause.
- **Opus outage.** A desktop-app update on 20 Oct moved the Claude CLI, so both Opus sleeves failed their 09:00 and 09:30 looks and placed nothing. The CLI search path was fixed and the loop restarted by 09:39; there was no catch-up look.
- **Margin rejections.** 18 orders on day 1 were rejected at the 2× limit, all from gpt-oss sleeves.
