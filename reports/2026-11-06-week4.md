## What the live test says about the models

- **Does training help? Not shown.** NAV9 trailed Base $100k by 8.8 points (−15.12% against −6.31%); NAV9 + ML2 led Base + ML2 $100k by 6.1 points (−3.00% against −9.08%). No gpt-oss base sleeve ran at $30,000 from a fresh start this week, so these pairs differ in size as well as training, and NAV9's Monday was partial. In simulation, F and H didn't beat base either. Over four weeks our trained or reserve checkpoints have trailed base in five of seven pairings.
- **Does the forecast tool help? The "+ ML2" sleeve led its twin in three of four pairs:** Sonnet by 5.4 points, Haiku by 1.9, NAV9 by 12.1; it trailed in the $100k base pair by 2.8 points. Between identical base runs at $30,000 we measured $1,700–2,100 a week of noise (5.7–7.0% of NAV), so the Sonnet and Haiku gaps are inside it and NAV9's is larger; that noise hasn't been measured at $100,000. Base + ML2 has no twin since week 3, and its week is on a carried book, so it says nothing about the tool. The replays found no final order the tool caused.
- **Claude against gpt-oss.** All four Claude sleeves did better than the four untrained gpt-oss sleeves (−4.66% to +0.72% against −9.08% to −6.31%). They traded far less: 22–128 orders and $9–45 of commission, against 406–455 orders and $331–353 for the base gpt-oss sleeves. Claude orders went out about 6 minutes after their decision this week, against about 1.6 before, because the gpt-oss chain now has five sleeves (estimated). Over weeks 2 and 3 the reverse held: Opus trailed Base + ML.
- **Against SPY, cash and the goal.** No sleeve reached the goal. Sonnet + ML2 beat SPY and cash; Haiku + ML2 beat SPY. Every gpt-oss sleeve lost more than SPY.
- **Sample size.** Each sleeve's week is one draw. The gpt-oss sleeves held similar semiconductor-short books into Friday, so their losses are largely one shared position.

## What we learned

1. **Base + ML2's lead didn't last.** After +12.20% in week 3 it lost 8.40% this week, most of it on Friday. Over two weeks it is +2.77% against SPY's −3.24%. The win/loss study had found week 3's gain to be exposure timing, and this week's loss is the same kind of bet going the other way.
2. **Training on base's own samples didn't beat base.** F and H, both SFT, failed or weren't shown, and the hold rule didn't carry. The re-samples of a look rarely contain a better decision.
3. **No direction signal yet.** SD1 joins F8 and seven direction model families in failing after costs.
4. **The tool changes research, not trades.** The replays credit it with sending the model to BA's news once; no trade.
5. **Trading less was worth more this week.** The Claude sleeves placed a fifth to a twentieth of the gpt-oss orders and lost less. One week; the Opus sleeves' weeks 2 and 3 went the other way.
