# Week 1: three traders on a live paper account (14–16 October 2026)

*Trader Training, written 21 October 2026.*

## Setup

- **Three sleeves of $30,000** on one IBKR paper account:
  - **base:** gpt-oss-120b, untrained;
  - **rl1:** gpt-oss-120b with our LoRA, training step 30 (see the [training report](2026-10-18-training-rl1-to-rl3.md));
  - **opus:** Claude Opus 5.5, used as it comes, as a reference.
- **Looks:** hourly from 09:00 to 15:00 New York time, with research tools (quotes, news, articles, market tape, and from 15 Oct a stock scanner).
- **First live day:** 14 October. Every sleeve placed orders that filled at the open.

## Results

Each sleeve's value at the 4:15 pm record, from its own fills, net of commissions:

| Base | $29,623 | $29,765 | $28,842 | **−3.86%** | $46.77 |

All three lost money; cash beat them all. SPY fell 0.78% from the 14 Oct close; we did not record SPY at the 14 Oct open, so there is no clean SPY figure for the whole week.

## Behaviour

On 15 October, with identical tools and market:

- **Opus** built a diversified, unlevered book on day 1 (large caps plus about a third in SPY) and placed 2 orders on day 2.
- **Base** traded at every look (16 orders), put about half its sleeve in one stock (ServiceNow), shorted Intel, and ran about 1.4× gross exposure.
- **rl1** traded the most (21 orders) and researched the most (51 lookups, 23 of them headlines). The scanner led it to five small, lesser-known stocks; one (NBIZ, bought at $34.90) became its largest loss.

The contrast in activity was the clearest finding of week 1. In week 2 it did not hold: see the [week 2 report](2026-10-20-week2-days1-2.md).

## What went wrong, and what changed

- **The loop died with a Claude session** and lost the 16 Oct looks from 08:15 to 11:00. Processes a Claude session starts die when that session restarts. The loop is now started detached through Windows WMI.
- **No margin limit at first.** Base and rl1 exceeded 2× gross. Clayton chose a margin call (cut to 1.95×, largest positions first), and the 2× limit is now enforced at order time, as in the simulator.
- **Changes for week 2** (made on 16–17 Oct, at Clayton's request or from these results): looks every 30 minutes until 15:30; notes a model writes at one look shown at the next; a `stats` tool; a weekly goal shown in the prompt; every model at its maximum reasoning effort; and an ML-forecast twin of each sleeve.
